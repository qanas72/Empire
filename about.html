01 · Purpose

Our Mission

Build a serious, welcoming digital community where members can create, connect and participate in a transparent platform economy.

One connected platform

Community publications, creator videos, user profiles, messaging, notifications, membership and wallet tools in one place.

Creator participation

Transparent development

02 · Community

A place to connect

EMPIRE brings social and community features together.

Publications & feed

Members can share text and image publications in the community feed. Regular and VIP members can participate; community posts are published directly, subject to platform rules and moderation.

Profiles & connections

Member profiles can show profile photos, publications, membership status and online/offline presence. Follow and follow-back connections help members discover one another. Administrator profiles are kept private from ordinary members.

Messaging & notifications

Members can communicate through EMPIRE messaging and receive activity notifications. The current messaging charge is 3 EMPC per message for both regular and VIP members; the fee is transferred to the platform treasury, not burned.

Community reactions

Eligible members can react to publications and videos with likes or dislikes. A like may qualify the creator for an EMPC reward when the relevant reward setting is enabled and treasury funds are available. Dislikes do not generate a like reward.

03 · Creators

Videos & publishing

AVAILABLE

Video library

EMPIRE supports free and paid video listings. Public videos are subject to the platform's approval and access rules.

VIP ACCESS

VIP video publishing

Active VIP members can submit videos for administrator review. A video becomes publicly available after approval. The current publishing fee is 50 EMPC per submitted video.

AVAILABLE

Text and image posts are available to regular and VIP members. Members can report content that may violate platform rules for administrator review.

05 · Digital economy

EmpireCoin (EMPC)

EMPC is the platform's internal accounting and reward unit.

INTERNAL LEDGER

What EMPC represents

EMPC balances and transfers are recorded in EMPIRE's internal wallet and transaction ledger. The initial treasury ledger allocation was set at 700,000,000 EMPC. This internal accounting balance is not proof of a dollar reserve, external backing or a live blockchain token.

Reference price

The initial internal reference price was set at $0.00076 per EMPC, with a planned increase of $0.0005 every three months. This is an administrator-configured reference value, not a guaranteed market price or promise of redemption.

Wallet & history

Members can view their EMPC balance and transaction history in the wallet area. Rewards, fees and transfers should be reflected in the ledger for traceability.

Platform fees

Current configured charges include 3 EMPC per message for every member and 50 EMPC for each VIP video submission. These fees can be reviewed and adjusted by administrators as the economy evolves.

06 · Participation

Rewards & referrals

Reward rules are subject to eligibility checks, administrator settings and available treasury funds.

Verified sign-up

A 10 EMPC sign-up reward is configured after a new member confirms their email, subject to reward settings and anti-abuse checks.

Community engagement

An eligible like can generate a 1 EMPC reward for the content creator when the reward is enabled. Dislikes do not earn this reward. Rewards are paid from the internal EMPC treasury, not created as external cash.

Referral rewards

A regular member who refers an eligible new member can receive 50 EMPC. An active VIP referrer can receive 100 EMPC. The referred member must confirm their email and complete a confirmed EMPC purchase of at least $2 for the referral reward to qualify.

Ad activity & Payout Points

Valid ad engagement can earn Payout Points according to administrator-configured settings. Payout Points are a separate withdrawal-limit measure; they are not EMPC and are not a cash balance.

07 · Giveaways

Official EMPIRE raffles

Raffles are managed by administrators and show their own prize details, conditions, status and winners.

Entry eligibility

The current raffle rule requires a confirmed EMPC purchase worth at least $5 and keeping at least the EMPC equivalent of $5 in the member's wallet. The public raffle page displays participant progress toward the configured target, currently designed around a 50-participant draw.

Prize structure

Each raffle has three administrator-defined main prizes. The current raffle page also displays 18 additional EMPC awards, from 6,600 down to 1,500 EMPC, totaling 72,900 EMPC. EMPC awards are credited from the internal treasury ledger.

VIP gifts

Administrators can grant a selected VIP plan as a complimentary membership, including as a raffle prize. Gifted membership is recorded in the VIP administration log.

Winners & transparency

Completed raffles display winner information and prize descriptions. Members should always read the conditions shown on the individual raffle before joining.

08 · Payments & withdrawals

Payments, balances & withdrawals

CURRENT DISPLAY

VIP payments

VIP plans are currently displayed in USDT and are activated after payment verification and administrator approval. Payment routes are subject to the options enabled in the live payment flow.

WITHDRAWAL RULES

USDT withdrawals

The configured withdrawal rules include a $10 minimum, one request per member per day, and a 5% service deduction so the member receives 95% of the requested gross amount, subject to approval and available USDT reserve.

Payout Points

Payout Points determine withdrawal limits and eligibility under the configured rules. They are not deducted as a percentage fee and are not USDT. A withdrawal cannot be paid unless the platform has sufficient real USDT reserves.

PLANNED · NOT A GUARANTEE

EMPC conversion & exchange

A future conversion design has been discussed: active VIP eligibility, a minimum of 15,000 EMPC, a 10% conversion fee, and crediting net USDT only when real USDT reserves are available. A separate planned sell eligibility rule requires holding at least 150,000 EMPC for one year. These features must not be treated as live or guaranteed until officially launched.

The EMPC reference price and internal treasury balance do not mean EMPC is backed by USDT. EMPC-to-USDT conversion or withdrawal must never be promised or credited without verified real USDT reserves and an operational payment process.
09 · Trust & safety

Account safety & moderation

Community standards

Members are expected to publish lawful, respectful content and follow EMPIRE's rules. Report tools help flag potentially prohibited or harmful material for review.

Account enforcement

Administrators can apply account suspensions for rule violations. Suspension is not account deletion and must not erase a member's wallet balance or transaction history. Account deletion is initiated by the account owner.

Payment awareness

Always verify the selected asset, network, amount and destination before making a payment. Never send funds to an address that is not displayed by the official EMPIRE payment flow.

Clear feature status

Features marked as planned are not available for use until their official release. Do not interpret roadmap descriptions or internal reference prices as investment advice or guaranteed returns.
